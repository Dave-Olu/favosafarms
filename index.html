About Favosa Farms

Built on the farm, not just for it

Favosa Farms was founded on a commitment to quality, transparency, and care. Starting as a small poultry and fish farm, we established one non-negotiable standard: every product we offer must be good enough for our own families.

That standard continues to define us. We raise healthy poultry, produce farm-fresh fish, and provide trusted pet care services while partnering with households, restaurants, and smallholder farmers. Our goal is simple: to deliver food people can trust and knowledge that creates lasting value.

What we do

Four ways we keep you and your farm healthy

Poultry rearing

FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot

What areas do you deliver to?

We currently deliver across Yenagoa and surrounding communities in Bayelsa State. Orders are fulfilled within 7 days of placement. Contact us to confirm availability in your specific area.

Do you deliver fish live or processed?

Both. You can specify at the time of order. Live catfish and tilapia are transported in aerated water. Processed fish is cleaned, dressed and packaged to your preference — whole, gutted or filleted.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Minimum orders vary by product. For fish, we typically start from 5 kg. For poultry, a minimum of 2 birds. Eggs are sold by the crate (30 eggs). Reach out via WhatsApp or phone and we'll work with your needs.

Are your products certified organic?

All our produce is raised without synthetic drugs, growth hormones or chemical feed additives. While we operate to organic standards, we are a small farm and do not yet hold a formal certification body stamp.

How do I book a farm consultation?

Use the contact form below, or reach us directly via WhatsApp or phone. Sessions can be conducted in person on-farm or remotely depending on your location. The first consultation call is free.

Do you accept bulk and restaurant orders?

Yes — we supply restaurants, hotels and caterers on a regular schedule. Get in touch to discuss volume pricing and recurring delivery arrangements.
