About Favosa Farms

Built on the farm, not just for it

Favosa Farms was founded on a commitment to quality, transparency, and care. Starting as a small poultry and fish farm, we established one non-negotiable standard: every product we offer must be good enough for our own families.

That standard continues to define us. We raise healthy poultry, produce farm-fresh fish, and provide trusted pet care services while partnering with households, restaurants, and smallholder farmers. Our goal is simple: to deliver food people can trust and knowledge that creates lasting value.

What we do

Four ways we keep you and your farm healthy

Poultry rearing

Free-range and deep-litter birds raised on clean feed, with full vaccination records for every batch we sell.

Fish farming

Catfish and tilapia grown in monitored ponds, harvested fresh and delivered live or processed to order.

Healthy produce

Eggs, dressed poultry and fresh fish, handled and stored the way we'd want them handled for our own kitchen.

Pet care

Feeding, grooming and basic health checks for dogs, so the animals in your home get the same standard of care.

Agric consultation

One-on-one guidance on poultry, fish or livestock setup — from site planning to your first successful harvest.

Farm-to-door delivery

Scheduled delivery routes across the state, so fresh produce reaches you the same day it leaves the farm.
